Job Title: Machine Learning Engineer (NLP)

We are looking for a Machine Learning Engineer with strong experience in Natural
Language Processing to join our data science team. The ideal candidate has
3+ years of experience building and deploying NLP models in production.

Requirements:
- Strong proficiency in Python
- Hands-on experience with NLP libraries such as spaCy, NLTK, or Hugging Face Transformers
- Experience with machine learning frameworks (scikit-learn, PyTorch, or TensorFlow)
- Solid understanding of data analysis using Pandas and NumPy
- Experience deploying models to cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or related field
- Strong communication skills and ability to work cross-functionally

Nice to have:
- Experience with Docker and Kubernetes
- Prior experience mentoring junior engineers or leading small teams
